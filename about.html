ABOUT US

At the Hummingbird Cafe, our team is composed of passionate, caring, and creative individuals who are dedicated to making every visit special. We work together to create a warm, relaxing environment where our guests can unwind and enjoy life’s simple pleasures. Above all, we are deeply committed to fostering a sense of community, where friendships grow, and laughter flows freely over a cup of coffee. Whether you're here for a quiet moment or to connect with others, we’re honored to be part of your day.

Jessica Houghton, Founder and CEO

The dream of owning and operating a café has been close to the hearts of Jessica Houghton, her daughters Cali and Kelsie Minkie, and their grandmother Renee Houghton for as long as they can remember. Inspired by the hummingbird—one of nature’s most resilient creatures—they set out to create a space that embodies the strength, persistence, and resilience the bird represents. Though small, the hummingbird is a symbol of hope and transformation, and it is our deepest wish that the Hummingbird Cafe serves as an oasis for those seeking a place of change, connection, and renewal.
